### 🧭 What I work on

I sit half in **engineering** and half in **research** — building the systems that make ML work in practice, and studying whether the results we get from those systems can actually be trusted.

- 🔬 **Data & evaluation** — measuring what models really do, and where benchmarks quietly break down.
- 🤖 **Applied ML** — turning models into things people can use, not just numbers in a paper.
- 🛡️ **AI safety** — integrity, threat modeling, and trustworthy evaluation of agentic systems.
- ⚙️ **AI infrastructure** — the pipelines, tooling, and plumbing that hold the above together.

---

### 🚀 Featured work

- **[monitorstress](https://github.com/tianyi-zhang-02/monitorstress)** — Multi-layer audit framework for **agent benchmark integrity**. Research meets tooling: catching when eval results don't mean what they claim to.
- **[tracker](https://github.com/tianyi-zhang-02/tracker)** — Mobile-first PWA for tracking net worth and investments, built with a **hostile-reader threat model**. Applied engineering with a security mindset.
- **[ACEBench](https://github.com/tianyi-zhang-02/ACEBench)** — Exploring how to improve and evaluate the skills of large language models.
